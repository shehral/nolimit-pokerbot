# Double board
Two five-card boards are dealt together (flop, turn, river on both). Same hole cards, same betting. At showdown every pot
(side pots too) is split in halves: first half to the best hand on board 1, second half to the best hand on board 2 (odd
chip goes to board 1). Ties split within a half. `state["board"]` is board 1; `state["boards"]` has both.
